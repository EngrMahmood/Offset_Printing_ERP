How these numbers are worked out

  • Tickets reported in the period are counted; cancelled tickets are excluded.
  • Downtime is time the machine was unavailable, clipped to the period; a machine still down is counted up to now.
  • MTTR is the average length of breakdown downtime that has ended (it measures time out of production, not technician hours). Downtime still open is left out until it closes.
  • Repairing / waiting come from each ticket's status history (In Progress versus Awaiting Parts / Vendor).
  • PM compliance is preventive tickets raised in the period that reached Verified or Closed.
  • MTBF is not shown: it needs reliable machine operating hours, which the ERP does not record.
