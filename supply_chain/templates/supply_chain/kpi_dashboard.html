KPI Reference

Classification & Formula Logic

ABC
Issuance × Unit Cost — top 70% A, next 20% B, last 10% C
FSN
Fast ≤30d, Slow 31–90d, Non-moving >90d since last issuance
ROP
(Daily Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
Turnover
Total Issued ÷ Average Inventory
