Master Data

Central management for ERP dropdown and reference values

Use this screen to correct or maintain master values used across planning, production, and job cards. Department and delivery location names are auto-synced from planning jobs and PO intake; product types are auto-synced from SKU master recipes when you open this page. Machine print sizes are entered in inches (matching how sheet sizes are recorded on planning jobs) and stored internally in mm - no manual conversion needed.
