Planning Workspace

Follow the steps from left to right. Start at Step 1 and continue until job card release. Each section is grouped by workflow stage to avoid confusion.

How To Use This Screen

  • Step 1: PO Intake - Upload or manually enter PO.
  • Step 2: SKU Master & New SKU - Check SKU existence and create missing master data.
  • Step 3: Planning Jobs - Planner fills planning-only fields and creates Job Card.
  • Step 4: Job Card Approval - QC reviews and approves Job Card.
  • Step 5: Production Scan - Production scans approved Job Card and starts execution.
